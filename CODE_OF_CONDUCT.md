# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the ModelPlay community and its communities will be treated with respect and dignity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful**: Using welcoming and inclusive language
- **Being considerate**: Think about how your words and actions affect others
- **Being honest**: Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully
- **Being collaborative**: Work together to solve problems and improve the project
- **Being supportive**: Help others learn and grow
- **Being patient**: Understand that people have different backgrounds and expertise levels

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination based on any characteristic
- Offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, disability, or other personal characteristics
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Deliberate disruption of conversations or activities
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including:
- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Community forums and chat channels
- Events and conferences
- Social media representations of the project

It also applies when someone is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it by contacting the project maintainers at:
- Opening a private issue on GitHub
- Emailing the project lead

**All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.**

### Investigation and Resolution

1. **Investigation**: The project team will promptly investigate the report
2. **Response**: The person who reported will be informed of the investigation outcome
3. **Action**: Appropriate actions will be taken, which may include:
   - A warning or request to stop the behavior
   - Temporary or permanent ban from community spaces
   - Other remedial actions as deemed necessary

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
- A private warning from community leaders
- Temporary suspension from community participation
- Permanent removal from the community
- Other appropriate actions
